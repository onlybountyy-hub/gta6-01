SOLANO HEAT is an original open-world crime game set in the sun-soaked coastal city of Solano. Explore a seeded procedural city of twelve-by-twelve blocks on foot and by car, steal and drive traffic cars, dodge or fight a five-star police response with a bust meter, and work a five-mission story chain for fixer Lola Marchetti — from a boosted car and a harbor package run, through a timed getaway and a warehouse shootout, to a final four-star escape across the city. It features traffic and pedestrian AI, hitscan pistol combat, two spray shops that clear your wanted level, a full HUD with a live canvas minimap, synthesized WebAudio sound and music, and local saves. Built with Three.js and plain static files; it plays fully offline in desktop Chrome.

The expansion pass deepened every system: buildings gained stepped crowns, rooftop clutter, awnings and shopfront signs; the park grew paths, a bandstand, flower beds and a statue; the harbour gained stacked containers, a marina of bobbing boats and a banded lighthouse; and a slow day cycle swings the light from noon to golden hour, switching car headlights on as it dips. Traffic now mixes sedans, delivery vans and pickups that brake-light and honk in gridlock, and pedestrians come in walkers, joggers, phone-lookers and chatting pairs. Between Lola's jobs you can run taxi fares for waiting passengers, chase timed stash drops phoned in by a tipster, enter the five-checkpoint Solano Sprint street race, and hunt 18 hidden packages. Combat gained muzzle flashes, ejecting shell casings and impact sparks; driving gained skid marks, tyre screech, camera shake on impacts and faster police interceptors at four stars and up.
## Run it

mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts && node serve.ts
